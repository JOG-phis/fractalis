{{< cita autor="Albert Einstein" fuente="Interview with William Miller, *LIFE* magazine, 1955" >}}The important thing is not to stop questioning.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Albert Einstein" fuente="Interview in *The Saturday Evening Post*, 1929" >}}Imagination is more important than knowledge.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Isaac Newton" fuente="Letter to Robert Hooke, 1676" >}}If I have seen further it is by standing on the shoulders of giants.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Marie Curie" fuente="Letter to her brother Józef, 1894" >}}One never notices what has been done; one can only see what remains to be done.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Richard Feynman" fuente="Written on his blackboard, 1988" >}}What I cannot create, I do not understand.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Richard Feynman" fuente="“Cargo Cult Science” commencement address, Caltech, 1974" >}}The first principle is that you must not fool yourself, and you are the easiest person to fool.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Rosalind Franklin" fuente="Letter to her father, c. 1940" >}}Science and everyday life cannot and should not be separated.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Carl Sagan" fuente="“Why We Need To Understand Science”, 1990" >}}Science is a way of thinking much more than it is a body of knowledge.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Plutarch" fuente="*On Listening* (usual paraphrase)" >}}The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Seneca" fuente="*Moral Letters to Lucilius*, 7.8" >}}Men learn while they teach.{{< /cita >}}

{{< cita autor="Francisco de Goya" fuente="Bordeaux Album, no. 54, c. 1826" >}}I am still learning.{{< /cita >}}
