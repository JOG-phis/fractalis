### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="4" pista="\(\Delta x = L - L_0\). In b), \(L = L_0 + \Delta x\)." solucion="a) \(\Delta x = 0.08\) m; \(k = 50\) N/m. b) 29 cm. c) 1.5 N." >}}
A spring is 15 cm long. When it is pulled with a force of 4 N, it becomes 23 cm long. a) Calculate its spring constant. b) How long will it be if we pull with 7 N? c) What force is needed to make it 18 cm long?

We push a 50 kg wardrobe, initially at rest, with a horizontal force of 296 N. The coefficient of friction with the floor is 0.4. a) Draw the forces and calculate the friction, the resultant force and the acceleration. b) What is its velocity after 4 s? How far has it moved?
