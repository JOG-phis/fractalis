### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="19" pista="Add up the electrons to get \(Z\). The number of shells is the period." solucion="a) 16. b) Group 16, period 3: sulfur (S). c) Non-metal. d) \(\ce{S^{2-}}\); like argon." >}}
A neutral atom has its electrons arranged like this: 2, 8, 6. a) What is its atomic number? b) Which group and period is it in? Which element is it? c) Is it a metal or a non-metal? d) Which ion does it form? Which noble gas does that ion resemble?

Write the formula of the compound formed by these ions: a) \(\ce{Na+}\) and \(\ce{Br-}\); b) \(\ce{Ca^{2+}}\) and \(\ce{O^{2-}}\); c) \(\ce{Mg^{2+}}\) and \(\ce{Cl-}\); d) \(\ce{Li+}\) and \(\ce{O^{2-}}\); e) \(\ce{Al^{3+}}\) and \(\ce{Cl-}\); f) \(\ce{K+}\) and \(\ce{N^{3-}}\); g) \(\ce{Ca^{2+}}\) and \(\ce{N^{3-}}\); h) \(\ce{Al^{3+}}\) and \(\ce{S^{2-}}\).
