With the PhET simulation **Isotopes and Atomic Mass** we build the isotopes of the first elements and find out where the atomic mass in the periodic table comes from.
{{< /lead >}}

- **Exploring isotopes:** mass number, isotopes of hydrogen and helium.
- **Calculating the relative mass:** a guided example with hydrogen and another with lithium, checked in the «Mixtures» screen.
- **Predict, calculate and check:** boron, carbon and nitrogen.
- **Special cases and final challenge:** elements with only one stable isotope, carbon-14 and a made-to-measure mixture of sulfur isotopes.
