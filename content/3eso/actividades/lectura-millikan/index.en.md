Robert Millikan measured the charge of the electron with drops of oil and won the Nobel Prize in 1923. Years later it emerged that he had not published all his measurements. Was it fraud?
{{< /lead >}}

A reading about the **charge of the electron** and **honesty in science**, with questions **before**, **during** and **after** reading: some about the graph of the drops Millikan used and the ones he left out, and others asking for an opinion on scientific fraud.
