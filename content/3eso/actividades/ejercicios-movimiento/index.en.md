### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="11" pista="When it stops, \(v = 0\). If it is braking, \(a < 0\)." solucion="a) 72 km/h = 20 m/s; with \(v^2 - v_0^2 = 2\,a\,\Delta x\), it travels 40 m before stopping: since 40 m > 35 m, it crashes. b) \(a = -5.71\,\text{m/s}^2\)." >}}
A car is travelling at 72 km/h when the driver sees an obstacle 35 m ahead and brakes with an acceleration of \(-5\,\text{m/s}^2\). a) Does it hit the obstacle? b) What braking acceleration would it have needed to stop just in front of it?
{{< /ejercicio >}}

{{< ejercicio n="14" pista="\(y_0 = 78.4\) m; \(v_0 = 0\); on the ground, \(y = 0\)." solucion="It takes 4 s and hits the ground at \(v = -39.2\) m/s (the sign shows it is moving downwards)." >}}
A ball is dropped from a roof 78.4 m high. How long does it take to reach the ground? At what velocity does it hit the ground?
{{< /ejercicio >}}
