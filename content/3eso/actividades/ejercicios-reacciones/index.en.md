### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="3" pista="Order: metals, other non-metals, H and O. If you get a decimal, multiply everything by 2." solucion="a) \(\ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}\). b) \(\ce{N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3}\). c) \(\ce{2Na + Cl2 -> 2NaCl}\). d) \(\ce{2Mg + O2 -> 2MgO}\). e) \(\ce{2KClO3 -> 2KCl + 3O2}\). f) \(\ce{4Al + 3O2 -> 2Al2O3}\)." >}}
Balance these chemical equations: a) \(\ce{H2 + O2 -> H2O}\); b) \(\ce{N2 + H2 -> NH3}\); c) \(\ce{Na + Cl2 -> NaCl}\); d) \(\ce{Mg + O2 -> MgO}\); e) \(\ce{KClO3 -> KCl + O2}\); f) \(\ce{Al + O2 -> Al2O3}\).
{{< /ejercicio >}}

{{< ejercicio n="23" pista="Balance it first. For every 2 mol of Mg, 1 mol of \(\ce{O2}\) reacts." solucion="a) \(\ce{2Mg + O2 -> 2MgO}\). 12 g of Mg is 0.5 mol. b) 0.25 mol of \(\ce{O2}\): 8 g. c) 0.5 mol of MgO: 20 g. d) \(12 + 8 = 20\) g." >}}
Magnesium burns with a very bright white light: \(\ce{Mg + O2 -> MgO}\). 12 g of magnesium are burnt. a) Balance the equation. b) What mass of oxygen reacts? c) What mass of magnesium oxide is formed? d) Check Lavoisier's law.
{{< /ejercicio >}}
