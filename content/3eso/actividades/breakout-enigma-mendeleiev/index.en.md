{{< lead >}}
D. M. has hidden his clues in the periodic table and in the last pages of his notebook. Only those who master the elements, bonding and chemical formulae will open the padlock on the box.
{{< /lead >}}

It revises topics 2 (**elements and compounds**) and 3 (**inorganic nomenclature**). Everything is done on paper: there is no hands-on stage.

**How it works.** The class is split into up to **8 teams**, each with its own fractal symbol. Each team opens the envelopes one at a time: to get the next one, they have to bring the **correct code** and their **reasoning**, written on the record sheet, to the control desk. Within each team there are four roles (reader, recorder, materials manager and spokesperson), which rotate at the start of stage 3, and each team has **two wildcards** to exchange for clues.

The PDF is the **team kit**: the record sheets (double-sided) and each team's cards and envelopes (single-sided, to cut out).

| Stage | Challenge |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Coordinates *(finding elements in the periodic table)* |
| 2 | The octet rule |
| 3 | Which bond? |
| 4 | Write the formula |
| 5 | Oxidation numbers |
