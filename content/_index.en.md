{{< por-que-fractalis >}}
A fractal is a shape that repeats its structure at every scale: a branch looks like the whole tree, and a stretch of coast like the whole coastline. Physics and Chemistry work the same way. The same ideas (matter, energy, interactions) keep coming back, from the atom to the galaxy and from 2 ESO to 2 Bachillerato, each time in more detail. That is why every year here has its own fractal.
{{< /por-que-fractalis >}}
