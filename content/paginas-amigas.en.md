When I started teaching I often felt lost and on my own. I don't know what would have become of my first years without the websites built by other colleagues in the field: resources that are usually better than mine, most of them shared under licenses that let you reuse their work as long as you credit the author. If physics and chemistry interest you, take the time to explore them thoroughly before continuing on here. In no particular order:
