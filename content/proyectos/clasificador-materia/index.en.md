{{< lead >}}
Is air matter? Is sand a solid or a liquid? Is tap water a pure substance or a mixture? Three levels for learning to sort matter through play. The app is in Spanish.
{{< /lead >}}

{{< app src="apps/clasificador-materia/" titulo="Clasificador de la materia" >}}

## How to use it in class

- **Level 1. Matter or not matter:** telling apart what has mass and takes up space from energy, forces or abstract ideas.
- **Level 2. States of matter:** solid, liquid or gas, including tricky cases such as sand, sponges and modelling clay.
- **Level 3. Sorting substances:** elements, compounds, and homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures.
- Each word has a **hint**, and the app saves the best score for each level in the browser. It has sound effects, which can be muted with the speaker button.
- It fits 2 ESO topics [1]({{< ref "2eso/apuntes/tema-1" >}}), [2]({{< ref "2eso/apuntes/tema-2" >}}) and [3]({{< ref "2eso/apuntes/tema-3" >}}): one level per topic, or all three as revision.
