## How to use it in class

- Each discovery (penicillin, the DNA double helix, Franklin's kite, photosynthesis, insulin, continental drift…) shows the five stages jumbled up: **observation, hypothesis, experimentation, analysis of results and communication**. Students have to put them in order.
- The app saves each student's progress in their browser, so they can stop halfway and carry on another day on the same device.
- It fits at the end of the scientific method section of [2 ESO, topic 1]({{< ref "2eso/apuntes/tema-1" >}}), or as a revision activity in pairs.
