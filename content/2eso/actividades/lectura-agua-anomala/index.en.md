Why doesn't the water at the bottom of lakes and rivers freeze even when the surface is frozen?
{{< /lead >}}

A reading about the **anomalous density of water**: unlike almost every other substance, water is denser at 4 °C than at 0 °C, and that is why ice floats. It includes a graph of the density of water against temperature and questions **before** and **after** reading.
