### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="1" pista="Does a new substance form? Then it is a chemical change." solucion="**Chemical:** a, c, e, g, h. **Physical:** b, d, f." >}}
State whether the following changes are physical or chemical.
a) Baking a sponge cake. b) Dissolving sugar in milk. c) Burning a sheet of paper. d) Breaking a plate. e) Milk going sour. f) Clothes drying on the line. g) A nail rusting outdoors. h) Digesting food.
{{< /ejercicio >}}

{{< ejercicio n="4" pista="There are six signs: colour, gas, solid (precipitate), temperature, light and smell." solucion="a) gas. b) colour. c) light. d) solid. e) temperature. f) smell. g) No: boiling water is a physical change." >}}
Which sign of a chemical reaction do we observe in each case?
a) When lemon juice is mixed with bicarbonate of soda, lots of bubbles come out. b) A ripe banana turns black. c) A lit sparkler shines very brightly. d) When two clear liquids are mixed, a yellow solid appears. e) Cement heats up by itself as it sets. f) Butter that has been open for a long time smells rancid. g) Bubbles also come out when water boils: is it a chemical reaction?
{{< /ejercicio >}}
