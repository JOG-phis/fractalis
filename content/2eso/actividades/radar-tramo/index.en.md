Average speed cameras photograph each car as it enters and leaves a stretch of road and work out its **average speed**. Who will get a fine?
{{< /lead >}}

- **Activity 1. Who will get a fine?** Using the times at which several vehicles pass through a 15 km stretch, calculate their average speed.
- **Activity 2. Five cars on one graph.** Interpret the position–time graph of five cars.
- **Activity 3. An accident report.** Using a data table and its graph, write the report.
