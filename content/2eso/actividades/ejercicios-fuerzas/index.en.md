### Two exercises to get started

{{< ejercicio n="1" pista="Before applying \(F = k \cdot \Delta x\), convert the extension to metres." solucion="a) \(k = \dfrac{4\,\text{N}}{0.20\,\text{m}} = 20\,\text{N/m}\). b) \(F = 20\,\text{N/m} \cdot 0.50\,\text{m} = 10\,\text{N}\)." >}}
A spring stretches 20 cm when a force of 4 N acts on it. a) What is its spring constant? b) What force must be applied to stretch it 50 cm?
{{< /ejercicio >}}

{{< ejercicio n="4" pista="The force stretching the spring is the weight: \(W = m \cdot g\), with the mass in kg." solucion="a) \(W = 3.92\,\text{N}\); \(k = 49\,\text{N/m}\). b) \(W = 5.88\,\text{N}\); it stretches 12 cm." >}}
When a 400 g object is hung from a spring, the spring stretches 8 cm. a) What is its spring constant? b) How much will it stretch if we hang a 600 g object from it?
{{< /ejercicio >}}
