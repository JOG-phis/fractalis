Each team is a **travel agency** organising a trip to New York. To plan everything, they need **conversion factors**.
{{< /lead >}}

The activity has six parts and an extension:

1. **How many kilometres will we travel?** The distance for each stretch is given in a different unit (mm, dam, hm, dm…) and they must be converted to kilometres to add them up.
2. **Packing the suitcase.**
3. **Hand luggage.**
4. **Liquids.**
5. **Times.**
6. **Temperature.**
