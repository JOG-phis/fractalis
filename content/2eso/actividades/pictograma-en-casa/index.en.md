Are there hazardous products in your home? Almost certainly. This task, designed for the virtual classroom, is about finding them.
{{< /lead >}}

1. Find a **hazard pictogram** on an everyday product in your home.
2. Take a **photo** and attach it to the task.
3. Answer: its **name**, **what it means** and **what to do**.
