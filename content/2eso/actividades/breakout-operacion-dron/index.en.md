A drone's flight log records all its movements. The teams will have to interpret it, measure speeds… and catch up with the drone.
{{< /lead >}}

**How it works.** The class is split into up to **8 teams**, each with its own fractal symbol. Each team opens the envelopes one at a time: to get the next one, they have to bring the **correct code** and their **reasoning**, written on the record sheet, to the control desk. Within each team there are four roles (reader, recorder, materials manager and spokesperson), which rotate halfway through the game, and each team has **two wildcards** to exchange for clues.

The PDF is the **team kit**: the record sheets (double-sided) and each team's cards and envelopes (single-sided, to cut out).

| Stage | Challenge |
| :---: | :--- |
| 1 | Which path? |
| 2 | The flight plan *(distance travelled and displacement)* |
| 3 | At a snail's pace *(hands-on, with a tape measure and a stopwatch)* |
| 4 | The flight graph |
| 5 | Catch the drone |
