With a **virtual lab** we measure the mass and volume of 12 objects, calculate their density and find out which objects float and why.

2. Does it float or sink in water?
3. We look for patterns.
4. Other liquids: we predict and check.
5. We change the density of the liquid.
6. Other substances.
7. Final conclusions.
